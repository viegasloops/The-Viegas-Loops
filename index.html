The Story Behind The Viegas Loop ✨

For the past 12 years, teaching has been my profession, but crocheting has been my lifelong sanctuary, hobby, and creative outlet ever since my school days—roughly 30 years of looping, stitching, and creating. What started as a childhood curiosity steadily grew into a deep-rooted passion, a quiet craft practiced through the rhythm of everyday life.

The Viegas Loop was born out of a desire to finally step out of the quiet corners of my craft room and share that decades-long journey with the world. It is a space dedicated to showcasing handcrafted art, slow fashion, and the joy of tactile creation—with the dream of turning a lifelong love affair with yarn into a meaningful business.

What began simply as an Instagram page to frequently post and document my handmade pieces has now blossomed into this digital storefront. Every single piece in our collection is crafted with patience, care, and the enduring belief that handmade items bring warmth and timelessness into our fast-paced world.

"Handmade with patience, designed for timelessness."

- LINET VIEGAS
